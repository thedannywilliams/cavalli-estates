The Residences

Stay at Cavalli Estates

Three private, beautifully appointed homes sit in the middle of Edna Valley — steps from the lavender fields and farm animals, framed by vineyards, citrus groves, and the San Luis Obispo mountains. The beach is just a ten-minute drive, with beautiful hiking all around.

Magical Experiences for All

Space to gather, room to wander

Find peace and tranquility across the estate — serene ponds alive with wildlife, wine at sunset or fishing at first light, bridle trails to walk, bike or ride, hot tubs and fire pits under the stars, and picnics in the cabanas among the lavender. The choices here are endless.

01 · Ranchhouse & Suites

The Ranchhouse

Rustic charm meets Edna Valley hospitality, set on 137 acres with views of ponds, mountains, and walking trails. Surrounded by pastures and friendly farm animals — ideal for families and nature lovers, with fire-pit nights and thirteen beds.
