The Vineyard House

Rise and shine in our Vineyard House, where the aroma of sun-kissed vines fills the air and the Edna Valley stretches out before you. Tucked among rolling vineyards, farmland, and friendly animals, it invites you to slow down, breathe deep, and take in the quiet beauty of California's Central Coast.

The Home

Made for gathering many

An outdoor living space, a pool and hot tub, a pickleball court, and room for seven — the Vineyard House is made for easy days and long, celebratory evenings among the vines. Where big families and close friends come together, celebrations spill easily from kitchen to pool to garden.
