The Equestrian Center

Where horses come home

Horses have always been at the heart of Cavalli. Across open pastures and the rolling Edna Valley hills, our equestrian center holds two callings under one sky — a peaceful retirement sanctuary for horses in their golden years, and a training center for horse and rider alike.

Two Callings, One Home

Room to retire,
room to grow

Whether a horse is winding down a lifetime of work or just beginning to find its stride, Cavalli offers the space, the care, and the calm to do it well. Both our retirement sanctuary and our training center share the same foundation — patient horsemanship, wide open land, and a genuine love of these animals.

Equine Retirement

A dignified retirement

For the horses who have given a lifetime, Cavalli offers a gentle place to rest. Wide pastures, attentive daily care, and the quiet company of the herd — a retirement lived out in comfort, safety, and grace.

Our sanctuary is built around the wellbeing of each horse, with room to roam and the unhurried rhythm of estate life. Retirement boarding options & care details available on inquiry.

Equestrian Training

A center for horse & rider

Our training center brings together thoughtful horsemanship and beautiful surroundings — a place to develop the partnership between horse and rider with patience, structure, and care.

From groundwork to the saddle, programs are shaped around each horse and each rider's goals. Disciplines, programs & lesson details to be confirmed.
