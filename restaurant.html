The Restaurant at Edna

From Michelin-starred chef Jeremy Fox, a table in the heart of Edna Valley, where the menu follows the harvest and the wine comes from the hills around you.

Reserve a Table Stay the Night

A Chef's Table in Wine Country

Grown here, made here, shared here

The Restaurant at Edna is guided by Partner and Culinary Director Jeremy Fox, one of California's most influential chefs, and his longstanding devotion to sustainability and seasonality. Executive Chef Matt Schaler, formerly of Birdie G's, leads the kitchen.

The cooking is hyper-local and changes with the season, often by the day, drawn from the same farms, fields, and vineyards that surround the valley. It is a warm room made for lingering, minutes from Cavalli Estates.
