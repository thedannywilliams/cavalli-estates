Getting Here

Easy to reach, worth the journey

Cavalli Estates sits just minutes from downtown San Luis Obispo and roughly fifteen minutes from San Luis Obispo County Regional Airport (SBP), making arrival simple whether you drive or fly.

Set on 137 private acres in the heart of Edna Valley, midway between Los Angeles and San Francisco along the Central Coast, the estate is an easy, beautiful arrival from anywhere in California.
