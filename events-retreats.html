Yoga in the Barn

Mats rolled out across the barn floor, string lights glowing in the trusses overhead, and morning light spilling through the timber. Our barn sessions bring teams together for guided yoga and breathwork in one of Edna Valley's most unforgettable rooms.

Sessions can be arranged for retreats of any size, complete with instructors, mats, and a catered brunch to follow.
