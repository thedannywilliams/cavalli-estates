The Setting

Edna Valley

One of California's most celebrated cool-climate wine regions, Edna Valley unfolds just beyond the estate gates — a patchwork of vineyards, rolling hills, and Central Coast light, minutes from downtown San Luis Obispo.

A Central Coast Treasure

Where the coast
meets the vine

Edna Valley is a designated American Viticultural Area shaped by its proximity to the Pacific — cool ocean breezes and long, sunlit days that lend themselves to elegant, Burgundian-style Chardonnay and Pinot Noir. Life here moves to a gentler rhythm: unhurried tasting-room afternoons, farm stands and roadside stalls, and hills that shift from mustard-gold to deep green with the seasons. It is the quiet, generous corner of the Central Coast that Cavalli calls home.

Wine Country

Celebrated wineries at your doorstep

Some of the Central Coast's most admired wineries and tasting rooms sit just minutes from the estate, many surrounded by the very vines that fill your glass. The valley has earned its reputation for cool-climate Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, and an afternoon here can wander from a hilltop terrace to a working-barn cellar.

Guests can arrange vineyard tours with transportation, so you can taste your way across the valley and leave the driving to someone else. Tell us your favourites and we'll help shape the day.

Getting Here

Easy to reach, worth the journey

Edna Valley sits just minutes from downtown San Luis Obispo and roughly 15 minutes from San Luis Obispo County Regional Airport (SBP), making arrival simple whether you drive or fly.

Positioned about mid-way between Los Angeles and San Francisco along the Central Coast, the valley is a natural stop on any California coastal journey — and a destination well worth staying for.
