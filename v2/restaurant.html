The Restaurant at Edna

Our Partners at the Table

Grown Here, Made Here, Shared Here

Gio and Lisa Coglitore, the family behind Cavalli Estates, are proud partners in The Restaurant at Edna: a fine dining room set among the vineyards at the heart of the valley. The restaurant is not on the Cavalli estate; it sits just down the road at Edna, about 2.4 miles from Cavalli, a six-minute drive.

The kitchen follows Jeremy Fox's longstanding devotion to sustainability and seasonality. The menu celebrates the agricultural abundance of the Central Coast and changes with the harvest, often by the day, while the wine list centers on the Central Coast with thoughtful selections from further afield.

Partner + Culinary Director

Jeremy Fox

One of the most influential chefs of his generation, Jeremy Fox is celebrated for ingredient-driven, zero-waste cooking. His first restaurant, Ubuntu in Napa Valley, earned a Michelin star and a place on The New York Times' list of the country's best restaurants, and Food & Wine named him a Best New Chef in 2008. He went on to create the Michelin-starred Rustic Canyon and the beloved Birdie G's in Santa Monica.

A seven-time James Beard Award nominee, he is the author of On Vegetables, a global bestseller and James Beard finalist that Food & Wine counts among the 35 best cookbooks of all time, and On Meat (2025).

Executive Chef

Matthew Schaler

Formerly Executive Chef of Birdie G's, Matthew Schaler joined Jeremy Fox on its opening team in 2019 and rose to lead the kitchen, helping shape its wood-grilled, house-fermented, deeply seasonal menu. StarChefs named him a Los Angeles Rising Star Chef in 2024, as much for his leadership as for his cooking.

At Edna he brings that same tenacity and care, with food made, in his words, "without pretense and with honesty."
