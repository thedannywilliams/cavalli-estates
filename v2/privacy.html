Your Information

Privacy & Cookies

A plain-language look at what this website collects, what stays on your device, and the choices you have.

Last updated November 18, 2026

What We Collect

When you send an inquiry or contact form, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, dates, and message. Forms are delivered to our team through our website host, Netlify. We use them only to answer you and plan your stay, event, or order.

Your Shopping Bag

Items you add to the bag are saved in your own browser (local storage) so they are still there when you come back. The bag stays on your device; nothing is sent to us until you check out.

Checkout and Payment

Orders from the Lavender Shop are completed on a secure checkout run by Shopify. Your payment and shipping details go directly to Shopify, and we receive the order to fill it. Shopify’s own privacy policy and cookies apply on its checkout pages.

Cookies on This Site

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics that follow you across the web.

  • Essential. Your shopping bag and your cookie choice are stored in your browser so the site works as expected. These are always on.
  • Google Maps. The estate maps are provided by Google, which may set cookies. Maps load only if you allow them, or when you tap “Show Map.”
  • Fonts. Our typefaces load from Google Fonts. Google receives your browser’s IP address to deliver them, but no cookies are set.

Your Choices

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie Settings, here or at the bottom of every page. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings, which empties your shopping bag.

California Privacy Rights

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-site advertising. California residents may ask what personal information we hold about them, and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Contact

Questions about this page or your information: call 805-242-1154, send a note through our contact form, or write to Cavalli Estates, 445 Green Gate Road, San Luis Obispo, CA 93401.
