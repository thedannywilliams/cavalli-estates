The Equestrian Center

Where Horses Come Home

Horses have always been at the heart of Cavalli. Across open pastures and the rolling Edna Valley hills, our equestrian center holds two callings under one sky: a training center for horse and rider alike, and a peaceful retirement sanctuary for horses in their golden years.

In association with San Luis Obispo Chapter · California Dressage Society

Two Callings, One Home

Room to Grow,
Room to Retire

Whether a horse is just beginning to find its stride or winding down a lifetime of work, Cavalli offers the space, the care, and the calm to do it well. Both our training center and our retirement sanctuary share the same foundation, patient horsemanship, wide open land, and a genuine love of these animals.

Meet the Trainers

Trainer & Instructor

Lisa Everett

  • Over forty years training horses and instructing riders
  • Classical Dressage & balanced centered riding
  • Dressage & Working Equitation competitor
Inquire about training with Lisa →

I have been training horses and instructing riders for over forty years. My approach combines the principles of Classical Dressage with the biomechanics of balanced centered riding. I strive to help riders build a strong partnership based on effective communication.

Over the years I have had the opportunity to work with many different breeds of horses and disciplines of riding and driving. In my younger days I worked with many off the track thoroughbreds, training them for their next careers in 3 Day Eventing and Hunter/Jumpers.

I have bred and raised several young horses of my own and started many young horses for my clients. I have also competed my own horses, bringing them up the levels in Dressage and Working Equitation as well as helping my students accomplish their own competition goals.

I enjoy sharing my knowledge with my students and tailoring my approach to their individual needs and aspirations. I believe in a holistic approach that addresses the horse’s physical and mental wellbeing.

I now proudly offer my services at Cavalli Estates.

California Dressage Society

The Equestrian Center at Cavalli Estates is proud to work in association with SLO-CDS, the Central Coast chapter devoted to fostering an interest in dressage: the gradual, harmonious development of horse and rider toward a perfect understanding.

Cavalli has opened its arenas and its tables to the chapter, hosting clinics with visiting masters and bringing members together for meetings on the estate.

    Retirement Sanctuary

    Cavalli Farms Retirement Sanctuary

    After years of dedication and hard work, every horse deserves a peaceful and comfortable retirement. At Cavalli Estates, we care for each horse as if they were our own, providing personalized attention, quality care, and a safe, serene environment.

    Whether your horse is ready to slow down, enjoy pasture life, or simply be cherished for all they have given, Cavalli offers a safe and loving place to call home.
