The Home

Perched Above the Vines

Surrounded by vineyards, pastures, and citrus groves, just minutes from the tasting rooms, with an outdoor fireplace, patios, and a pool for long golden afternoons. Six bedrooms and spa-like bathrooms sleep up to sixteen, making it perfect for bridal parties, family groups, or friends reconnecting over wine and sunshine.
