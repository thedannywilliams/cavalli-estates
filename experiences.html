The Estate

Experiences at Cavalli

One hundred and thirty-seven acres made for unhurried days. Fill an afternoon, or a long weekend, with everything the estate has to offer.

On the Water

Fishing ponds

Cast a line on the estate's stocked ponds and let the afternoon drift by. Serene water, resident wildlife, and the kind of quiet that is getting hard to find.

Stay steps from the water →

On Horseback

Bridle trails

Miles of gentle trails wind through the hills, made for wandering on horseback, on foot, or by bike. In spring, the mustard turns the whole valley gold.

Visit the Equestrian Center →

In the Branches

Treehouse dining

Share a catered meal from one of San Luis Obispo's best restaurants, served on our steel treehouse suspended in the trees. An unforgettable table for an unforgettable evening.

Arrange a dinner →

Under the Stars

Hot tubs & fire pits

End the day the right way: warm water, a crackling fire, and a sky full of stars. Every residence has its own way to unwind after dark.

Explore the residences →

In the Fields

Cabana picnics

Reserve a shaded cabana beside the lavender and picnic in the heart of the fields, with the fragrance of fifteen acres in bloom all around you.

Visit the Lavender Farm →

The Sanctuary

Meet the animals

Say hello to the Highland cattle, horses, alpaca, sheep, and hens. Every animal here is known by name, and they are always happy to see visitors.

Visit the sanctuary →
